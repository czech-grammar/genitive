# Personal Pronouns in Genitive

Personal pronouns in genitive are used for possession, after prepositions, and with verbs that require genitive.

---

## Quick Reference

| Person | Nominative | Genitive (full) | Genitive (short) |
|:-------|:-----------|:----------------|:-----------------|
| I | já | **mne** | **mě** |
| you (sg.) | ty | **tebe** | **tě** |
| he | on | **jeho/něho** | **ho/něj** |
| she | ona | **jí/ní** | **jí** |
| it | ono | **jeho/něho** | **ho** |
| we | my | **nás** | **nás** |
| you (pl.) | vy | **vás** | **vás** |
| they | oni/ony | **jich/nich** | **jich** |

---

## Short vs Long Forms

| | Short (unstressed) | Long (stressed) |
|:--|:-------------------|:----------------|
| me | **mě** | **mne** |
| you | **tě** | **tebe** |
| him/it | **ho** | **jeho** |
| her | **jí** | **jí** |
| us | **nás** | **nás** |
| you (pl) | **vás** | **vás** |
| them | **jich** | **jich** |

:::tip When to Use Which?
- **Short forms** = after prepositions, normal usage
- **Long forms** = emphasis, at start of clause
:::

---

## After Prepositions: Add N-

After prepositions, third-person pronouns add **n-**:

| Without preposition | After preposition |
|:--------------------|:------------------|
| Bojím se **ho**. | Bojím se kvůli **němu**. |
| Bojím se **jí**. | Jdu bez **ní**. |
| Bojím se **jich**. | Jdu vedle **nich**. |

### Examples with Prepositions

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| bez **něho/něj** | without him |
| bez **ní** | without her |
| bez **nich** | without them |
| od **něho** | from him |
| od **ní** | from her |
| u **něho** | at his place |
| u **ní** | at her place |
| vedle **mě** | next to me |
| kvůli **tobě** | because of you |

---

## First Person: Já → Mě/Mne

| Form | Usage | Example |
|:-----|:------|:--------|
| **mě** | after prepositions | bez mě, vedle mě |
| **mne** | emphatic, start of clause | Mne se neptej! |

### Examples

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| bez **mě** | without me |
| vedle **mě** | next to me |
| kvůli **mně** | because of me |
| u **mě** | at my place |
| od **mě** | from me |

---

## Second Person: Ty → Tě/Tebe

| Form | Usage | Example |
|:-----|:------|:--------|
| **tě** | after most prepositions | bez tě, vedle tě |
| **tebe** | emphatic | Kvůli tebe! |

### Examples

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| bez **tebe** | without you |
| vedle **tebe** | next to you |
| kvůli **tobě** | because of you |
| u **tebe** | at your place |
| od **tebe** | from you |

---

## Third Person: On/Ona/Oni

### He/It: On/Ono → Jeho/Ho/Něho

| Form | Usage | Example |
|:-----|:------|:--------|
| **ho** | with verbs | Bojím se ho. |
| **jeho** | emphatic | Jeho se nebojím! |
| **něho/něj** | after preposition | bez něho, vedle něj |

### She: Ona → Jí/Ní

| Form | Usage | Example |
|:-----|:------|:--------|
| **jí** | with verbs | Bojím se jí. |
| **ní** | after preposition | bez ní, vedle ní |

### They: Oni → Jich/Nich

| Form | Usage | Example |
|:-----|:------|:--------|
| **jich** | with verbs | Bojím se jich. |
| **nich** | after preposition | bez nich, vedle nich |

### Examples

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| bez **něho** | without him |
| u **něho** | at his place |
| od **něj** | from him |
| bez **ní** | without her |
| u **ní** | at her place |
| bez **nich** | without them |
| vedle **nich** | next to them |

---

## Reflexive: Se → Sebe

| Form | Usage | Example |
|:-----|:------|:--------|
| **sebe** | after prepositions, emphatic | Myslí jen na sebe. |

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Myslí jen na **sebe**. | He thinks only of himself. |
| Je to pro **sebe**? | Is it for yourself? |
| Bojí se **sebe** sama. | She's afraid of herself. |

---

## Possessive Pronouns (Genitive Form)

When you need to say "of mine," "of yours," etc.:

| Whose? | Genitive |
|:-------|:---------|
| my/mine | **mého/mé** |
| your/yours (sg.) | **tvého/tvé** |
| his | **jeho** |
| her/hers | **jejího/její** |
| our/ours | **našeho/naší** |
| your/yours (pl.) | **vašeho/vaší** |
| their/theirs | **jejich** |

## Common Phrases

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Bez tebe nemůžu žít. | I can't live without you. |
| U nás doma. | At our home. |
