# Common Uses of the Genitive

The Genitive has more uses than just prepositions. Here are all the contexts where you'll need it.

---

## 1. Possession (Of)

The most basic use - expressing **belonging** or **relationship**:

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| kniha **bratra** | brother's book |
| dům **rodičů** | parents' house |
| auto **otce** | father's car |
| konec **filmu** | end of the film |
| střed **města** | center of the city |
| autor **knihy** | author of the book |
| barva **auta** | color of the car |

:::tip Word Order
In Czech, the possessor comes **after** the thing possessed:
- kniha bratra (lit: book of-brother)
- NOT ~~bratra kniha~~
:::

---

## 2. Quantities & Numbers

### After Numbers 5+

Numbers 5 and higher require **genitive plural**:

| Number | Example |
|:-------|:--------|
| 5 | pět **studentů** |
| 6 | šest **žen** |
| 7 | sedm **měst** |
| 10 | deset **knih** |
| 20 | dvacet **korun** |
| 100 | sto **lidí** |

### Compare: 1-4 vs 5+

| Number | Case Used | Example |
|:-------|:----------|:--------|
| 1 | Nominative | jeden student |
| 2-4 | Nominative plural | dva studenti, tři studenti |
| 5+ | **Genitive plural** | pět student**ů** |

### Quantity Words

These words also require genitive:

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| mnoho | many | mnoho **lidí** |
| málo | few, little | málo **času** |
| hodně | a lot | hodně **peněz** |
| trochu | a little | trochu **vody** |
| několik | several | několik **minut** |
| kolik | how many | kolik **knih**? |
| tolik | so many | tolik **problémů** |
| dost | enough | dost **místa** |

---

## 3. Partitive (Part of Something)

When talking about **a portion** of something:

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| kousek **chleba** | a piece of bread |
| sklenice **vody** | a glass of water |
| šálek **kávy** | a cup of coffee |
| láhev **vína** | a bottle of wine |
| kilo **jablek** | a kilo of apples |
| trochu **mléka** | a little milk |
| půl **hodiny** | half an hour |

---

## 4. Negation

In some contexts, negated verbs take genitive instead of accusative:

| Affirmative (Accusative) | Negative (Genitive) |
|:-------------------------|:--------------------|
| Mám čas. | Nemám **času**. |
| Je tu voda. | Není tu **vody**. |
| Mám peníze. | Nemám **peněz**. |

:::info Note
This is more literary/formal. In everyday speech, accusative is often used even with negation:
- Nemám čas. (common)
- Nemám času. (formal/literary)
:::

---

## 5. Date Expressions

Dates use genitive:

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| prvního ledna | on January 1st |
| pátého března | on March 5th |
| do konce **roku** | by the end of the year |
| od začátku **měsíce** | since the beginning of the month |

---

## 6. Time Expressions

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Kolik je **hodin**? | What time is it? |
| pět **minut** | five minutes |
| dvacet **sekund** | twenty seconds |
| osm **hodin** ráno | eight o'clock in the morning |

---

## 7. Adjectives Requiring Genitive

Some adjectives require their complement in genitive:

| Adjective | Meaning | Example |
|:----------|:--------|:--------|
| plný | full of | plný **vody** |
| prázdný | empty of | prázdný **lidí** |
| hodný | worthy of | hodný **pozornosti** |
| schopný | capable of | schopný **všeho** |
| zbavený | rid of | zbavený **strachu** |

---

## 8. Verbs Requiring Genitive

Some verbs take genitive objects:

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| bát se | to be afraid of | Bojím se **psa**. |
| ptát se | to ask about | Ptám se **cesty**. |
| týkat se | to concern | Týká se **tebe**. |
| všímat si | to notice | Všímám si **detailů**. |
| vzdát se | to give up | Vzdal se **naděje**. |
| zbavit se | to get rid of | Zbavil se **problému**. |
| dotknout se | to touch | Dotkl se **zdi**. |
| chopit se | to seize | Chopil se **příležitosti**. |

### Examples

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Bojím se tmy. | I'm afraid of the dark. |
| Ptám se na cestu. | I'm asking for directions. |
| To se týká všech. | That concerns everyone. |
| Vzdal se kouření. | He gave up smoking. |

---

## 9. Comparisons (Than)

In formal/literary Czech, **než** (than) can be replaced with genitive:

| With než | With Genitive |
|:---------|:--------------|
| starší než já | starší **mě** |
| lepší než on | lepší **jeho** |

---

## Summary: When to Use Genitive

| Context | Example |
|:--------|:--------|
| **Possession** | kniha bratra |
| **After 5+** | pět studentů |
| **Quantities** | mnoho lidí |
| **Partitive** | sklenice vody |
| **Prepositions** | z Prahy, do školy |
| **Some verbs** | bát se psa |
| **Dates/Time** | pátého března |
| **Negation** (formal) | nemám času |
