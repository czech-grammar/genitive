Genitive Prepositions

The Genitive case is required after many common prepositions. These are some of the most frequently used prepositions in Czech.

Overview​

PrepositionMeaningExample
z/zefrom, out ofz Prahy (from Prague)
doto, into, untildo školy (to school)
odfrom, sinceod bratra (from brother)
bezwithoutbez cukru (without sugar)
uat, near, at someone's placeu doktora (at the doctor's)
vedlenext tovedle banky (next to the bank)
kolem/okoloaroundkolem domu (around the house)
místoinstead ofmísto kávy (instead of coffee)
běhemduringběhem léta (during summer)
kvůlibecause ofkvůli tobě (because of you)
kroměexcept, besideskromě mě (except me)
podleaccording to, alongpodle mapy (according to the map)
blízkonearblízko centra (near the center)
uprostředin the middle ofuprostřed města (in the middle of the city)

Z/Ze (From, Out of)​

Z (or ze before difficult consonant clusters) expresses origin or source.

CzechEnglish
Jsem z Prahy.I'm from Prague.
Vrátil se z práce.He returned from work.
Vyšel z domu.He left the house.
Sklenice z mléka.A glass made of milk.
Z čeho to je?What's it made of?

Z vs Ze​

Use ze when z would be hard to pronounce:

  • ze školy (from school)
  • ze Slovenska (from Slovakia)
  • ze dne na den (from day to day)

Do (To, Into, Until)​

Do expresses motion into or toward an enclosed space, or "until" for time.

CzechEnglish
Jdu do školy.I'm going to school.
Jdu do města.I'm going to town.
Jdu do obchodu.I'm going to the shop.
Přijdu do pěti.I'll come by five.
Od rána do večera.From morning to evening.
Do vs Na
  • Do + Genitive = into enclosed spaces (buildings, cities)
  • Na + Accusative = onto surfaces, events, open areas
DoNa
do školy (to school)na univerzitu (to university)
do obchodu (to the shop)na poštu (to the post office)
do města (to the city)na koncert (to the concert)

Od (From - Person/Time)​

Od expresses "from" a person or a starting point in time.

CzechEnglish
Dopis od bratra.A letter from (my) brother.
Dárek od kamaráda.A gift from a friend.
Od rána jsem tady.I've been here since morning.
Od pondělí do pátku.From Monday to Friday.
Od kdy jsi tady?Since when are you here?

Bez (Without)​

Bez means "without."

CzechEnglish
Káva bez cukru.Coffee without sugar.
Jsem bez peněz.I'm without money.
Bez problémů.No problem. (Without problems.)
Bez tebe nemůžu žít.I can't live without you.
Bez komentáře.No comment.

U (At, Near, At Someone's Place)​

U expresses location near something, or being at someone's place.

CzechEnglish
Jsem u doktora.I'm at the doctor's.
Čekám u vchodu.I'm waiting at the entrance.
Bydlím u rodičů.I live at my parents' place.
U nás doma.At our home.
Sedí u stolu.He's sitting at the table.

Location Prepositions​

Vedle (Next to)​

CzechEnglish
Vedle banky.Next to the bank.
Sedí vedle mě.He's sitting next to me.
Dům vedle školy.The house next to the school.

Kolem/Okolo (Around)​

CzechEnglish
Kolem domu.Around the house.
Okolo páté hodiny.Around five o'clock.
Chodí kolem mě.He walks around me.

Blízko (Near)​

CzechEnglish
Blízko centra.Near the center.
Bydlím blízko školy.I live near the school.

Uprostřed (In the middle of)​

CzechEnglish
Uprostřed města.In the middle of the city.
Uprostřed noci.In the middle of the night.

Time & Reason Prepositions​

Během (During)​

CzechEnglish
Během léta.During summer.
Během hodiny.During the hour.
Během dne.During the day.

Kvůli (Because of)​

CzechEnglish
Kvůli tobě.Because of you.
Kvůli práci.Because of work.
Kvůli počasí.Because of the weather.

Místo (Instead of)​

CzechEnglish
Místo kávy.Instead of coffee.
Místo mě.Instead of me.
Místo práce.Instead of work.

Other Important Prepositions​

Kromě (Except, Besides)​

CzechEnglish
Všichni kromě mě.Everyone except me.
Kromě toho.Besides that.

Podle (According to, Along)​

CzechEnglish
Podle mapy.According to the map.
Podle mě.In my opinion. (According to me.)
Jdi podle řeky.Go along the river.

Pomocí (With the help of)​

CzechEnglish
Pomocí počítače.With the help of a computer.
Pomocí slovníku.Using a dictionary.

Common Phrases​

CzechEnglish
Z celého srdce.From the whole heart. (Wholeheartedly.)
Bez pochyby.Without doubt.
Do konce roku.By the end of the year.
Od rána do večera.From morning to evening.
U nás.At our place.
Kvůli čemu?Because of what? / Why?
Místo toho.Instead of that.
Podle mého názoru.In my opinion.

Practice​

Complete with the correct preposition:

  1. Jsem ___ Prahy. (from Prague)
  2. Jdu ___ školy. (to school)
  3. Dopis ___ bratra. (from brother)
  4. Káva ___ cukru. (without sugar)
  5. ___ doktora. (at the doctor's)
  6. ___ banky. (next to the bank)
  7. ___ léta. (during summer)
  8. ___ tobě. (because of you)
  9. ___ mě. (except me)
  10. ___ mapy. (according to the map)
Check Answers
  1. Jsem z Prahy.
  2. Jdu do školy.
  3. Dopis od bratra.
  4. Káva bez cukru.
  5. U doktora.
  6. Vedle banky.
  7. Během léta.
  8. Kvůli tobě.
  9. Kromě mě.
  10. Podle mapy.
